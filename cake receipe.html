Fluffy Banana Pancakes Receipe

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 2 large eggs
  • 25g oats (optional, for thickness)

Instructions

  1. Mash: Mash the banana in a bowl until smooth.
  2. Mix: Whisk in the eggs (and oats) until fully combined.
  3. Cook: Fry small spoonfuls in a greased pan over medium heat for 2 minutes per side.
